Archive Paper

A family archive that reads like a well-kept album. Warm paper, ink-black type, one oxblood accent. Serif names give each person a sense of weight; everything else stays quiet and flat. References: Refero's warm-editorial styles (ElevenLabs, Perplexity, Slite), museum collection sites.
