Family history as a record of places and dates. A clean, cool, cartographic system: navy ink, a sea-green working colour and ochre map pins. References: Refero's blueprint and research-notebook styles (Ui, Inthememory, Sprig) and Gramps Web's map and timeline views, simplified.
