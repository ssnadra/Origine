# Origine

A private website where one extended family builds and views its family tree together and shares photos.

## Language

**Family site**:
The single shared tree together with everyone who can sign in to it. Origine hosts exactly one.
_Avoid_: Family unit, workspace, group

**Person**:
Anyone recorded in the tree, living or passed away, with or without an account.
_Avoid_: Individual, node, record, profile

**Member**:
A Person who has an account and can sign in.
_Avoid_: User, account holder

**Admin**:
A Member with extra powers over other Members' access. The founder of the Family site is the first Admin.
_Avoid_: Owner, moderator

**Invitation**:
A single-use link that lets one living Person without an account become a Member.
_Avoid_: Invite code, access request

## The tree

**Link**:
A recorded relationship between two People: a Parent link, a Partner link or a Sibling link.
_Avoid_: Connection, edge, relation

**Parent link**:
A link saying one Person is a parent of another, whether by birth or adoption. A Person has at most four parents.
_Avoid_: Birth link, adoption link

**Partner link**:
A link between two People who are or were a couple. It is either **current** or **former**.
_Avoid_: Marriage, spouse link, union, family

**Former partner**:
A partner the Person separated from or divorced. A partner who has died is still a current partner.
_Avoid_: Ex, late partner

**Siblings**:
Two People who share a recorded parent, or who are joined by a Sibling link.
_Avoid_: Brothers and sisters (as a term)

**Sibling link**:
A link saying two People are siblings even though no shared parent is recorded for them.
_Avoid_: Half-sibling link

**Half-siblings**:
Siblings who are known not to share every parent. They are recorded as a Sibling link that stays after a "No" answer to "Is [parent] also the parent of [sibling]?".

**Connected**:
The whole tree forms one piece: every Person can be reached from every other Person through links. Nothing may leave the tree in more than one piece.
_Avoid_: Linked, attached

## People

**Given names**:
A Person's first name or names, for example "Maria Elena".
_Avoid_: First name, forename

**Family name**:
A Person's current surname.
_Avoid_: Last name, surname (alone)

**Birth surname**:
The surname a Person was born with, when it differs from their family name.
_Avoid_: Maiden name

**Approximate date**:
A date known only as "about" a given year.
_Avoid_: Estimated, circa

**Passed away**:
A Person who has died. A Person counts as passed away when someone marks them so, or when they were born more than 110 years ago and have no death date. Everyone else is living.
_Avoid_: Deceased, dead

**Profile photo**:
A square crop of a Photo the Person appears in, used for their card and page.
_Avoid_: Avatar, portrait

## Changes

**Change**:
One attributed edit to the tree or its photos: who made it, when, and what it was before and after.
_Avoid_: Edit, revision, event

**Removed**:
Taken out of the tree or the photo grid by a Member, but still able to be restored.
_Avoid_: Deleted, archived
